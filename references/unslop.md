# Unslop

Edit only the requested text. Preserve its meaning, level of certainty, factual claims, and intended voice.

- Remove empty introductions, praise, generic conclusions, and phrases such as "it is important to note."
- Replace fancy synonyms with ordinary words where precision permits: "use," "help," and "is."
- Replace vague attributions and abstract claims with supporting facts, or flag the missing evidence. Do not invent sources.
- State the point directly. Remove rhetorical contrasts, forced lists of three, synonym cycling, and metaphors that obscure the mechanism.
- Reduce repeated bold labels, decorative headings, and punctuation used as a substitute for sentence structure. Keep formatting that helps readers scan.
- Split dense sentences, clarify pronouns, name the actor, and retain articles and verbs. Concision should reduce reading effort.

Leave technical terms, quotations, deliberate style choices, and useful nuance intact. Return the edited text; explain changes only when requested or when an ambiguity needs the author's decision. No mandatory banned-word catalog or changes to other skills.
